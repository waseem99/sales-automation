# Sales Navigator V5 — FinTech live pilot

## Objective

Validate that the campaign can repeatedly surface **commercially plausible FinTech buyers and partners** for Codistan's backend-operations platform and managed delivery offer.

This pilot is not approved by capture volume alone. It must demonstrate:

- stable person/profile identity and deduplication;
- credible buyer authority and account fit;
- enough evidence to prepare a relevant manual approach;
- a meaningful route to product, outsourcing, white-label or implementation work;
- zero automated LinkedIn or email action.

## Safety and operating boundary

Use a licensed, normal logged-in Sales Navigator session. Keep scheduled Sales Navigator capture disabled until the manual pilot passes. Run one registered search at a time during calibration.

The extension may open an inactive registered search tab and perform bounded scrolling. It must never save a lead, open profiles in bulk, connect, follow, send InMail, message, email, react, comment or bypass a LinkedIn control.

## Offer baseline

Campaign: **FinTech Backend Operations Platform**

Positioning used during discovery:

- backend operations and workflow platform;
- integrations and operational automation;
- managed software/product delivery;
- white-label, overflow and outsourcing partnership;
- AI-enabled operations only where the account evidence supports it.

Do not claim that a captured prospect requested this solution. Every record remains a cold prospect until independent buyer intent is found.

## Pilot search architecture

Create three separate lead searches. Do not combine all personas into one global search because that prevents useful calibration.

### Search 1 — Technology and product ownership

Target titles/functions:

- CTO, CIO or CPO;
- VP Engineering or VP Product;
- Head of Technology, Engineering, Product or Platform;
- Head of Integrations or API/Platform leadership;
- Founder/Co-Founder where the company is still founder-led.

Purpose: find buyers who can sponsor platform modernization, integrations, product delivery or an outsourced engineering pod.

### Search 2 — Operations and transformation ownership

Target titles/functions:

- COO;
- Head/Director of Operations;
- Head of Digital Transformation;
- Head of Business Operations;
- General Manager responsible for a digital financial product.

Purpose: find buyers with operational pain across onboarding, reconciliation, case management, compliance workflows, partner operations or internal process automation.

### Search 3 — Partnerships and delivery channels

Target titles/functions:

- Head/VP of Partnerships;
- Strategic Partnerships;
- Head of Integrations;
- Platform Partnerships;
- Technology Alliances;
- Founder/COO at a FinTech implementation or product company.

Purpose: find white-label, implementation, overflow, referral and market-entry partners rather than only direct end buyers.

## Account filters for the first wave

Start with separate searches for **UAE** and **United Kingdom** so geography performance remains measurable. Add Saudi Arabia as a separate second-wave search after the first 25 records are reviewed.

Use target account themes such as:

- FinTech;
- payments and payment infrastructure;
- digital banking and wallets;
- lending, NBFC, microfinance and alternative finance;
- remittance and cross-border payments;
- financial infrastructure, RegTech and operations platforms.

Prefer company headcount bands of **11–200** and **201–500** for the first pilot. Very small companies may lack budget; very large institutions may require a longer procurement route. Keep these bands in separate searches when result volume permits.

Prioritize visible relationship or timing signals where Sales Navigator makes them available:

- second-degree or TeamLink path;
- posted on LinkedIn;
- recent role change;
- recent account growth or a relevant account signal.

Do not use buyer-intent or category-intent filters unless the licensed Sales Navigator plan actually exposes them and the signal meaning is understood.

## Registration and first run

1. Install the V5 branch and reload LinkedIn extension version 1.4.1.
2. Confirm `http://127.0.0.1:8785/health` reports ready.
3. Keep **Scheduled capture enabled** switched off.
4. Build and refine one Sales Navigator lead search using the criteria above.
5. Leave the search tab open.
6. Open the LinkedIn extension → **Open Sales Navigator campaigns**.
7. Leave the URL field blank and click **Register search** to register the most recently used Sales Navigator search tab.
8. Click **Run this campaign now**.
9. Read the displayed per-search diagnostics before changing filters.
10. Run **Check Sales Navigator Pilot** from the desktop after each capture cycle.

## Technical acceptance gates

The local pilot checker must show:

- at least 25 unique prospects;
- at least 15 Priority A/B prospects;
- at least 95% canonical profile evidence;
- at least 80% current-role evidence;
- at least 80% company evidence;
- at least 95% campaign metadata coverage;
- cold-prospect/no-explicit-intent warning retained on at least 95% of records;
- zero external-action records.

Passing these gates means the dataset is ready for human commercial review. It does not approve outreach or scheduled operation.

## Human commercial review

Review at least 15 Priority A/B records. Score each from 0–2 on:

1. account fit for the selected FinTech offer;
2. authority to sponsor or influence the work;
3. plausible operational or platform problem;
4. relevant proof Codistan can credibly share;
5. realistic manual contact or relationship path.

Maximum score: 10.

Treat a record as commercially review-worthy at **7 or above**. Approve the search lane only when at least **60%** of the reviewed A/B sample reaches that threshold.

## Keep/change/stop rules

### Keep

Keep a search lane when:

- technical gates pass;
- at least 60% of the 15-record human sample is review-worthy;
- the proposed angle can be stated without inventing pain or buyer intent;
- the account size and geography fit Codistan's realistic closing path.

### Change

Change filters, campaign terms or scoring when:

- fewer than 15 A/B records emerge from 25 unique captures;
- role or company completeness is below 80%;
- the same non-buying persona repeatedly appears;
- the search produces correct industries but weak authority;
- strong prospects are being held in Research because visible evidence is incomplete.

### Stop

Stop a lane when:

- fewer than 40% of the human-reviewed A/B sample is commercially plausible after one filter revision;
- it consistently returns recruiters, consultants, job seekers or unrelated service vendors;
- account restrictions, checkpoints or unusual-activity warnings appear;
- the only available angle is generic staff augmentation with no credible differentiation.

## Information required before outreach calibration

Technical capture can proceed with the current campaign definition. Final outreach and proof selection require the following product inputs:

- product/platform name;
- three concrete backend workflows it solves;
- target customer type and preferred company size;
- deployment model: SaaS, white-label, custom implementation or hybrid;
- integration capabilities and supported systems;
- available demo, screenshots, architecture or case study;
- expected engagement/pricing range;
- strongest reason to buy rather than build internally.

Until these are supplied, draft outreach remains provisional and must not overstate product readiness or customer proof.
