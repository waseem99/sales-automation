# LinkedIn warm-intent contract

- Every captured LinkedIn candidate receives a structured category, reasons and retained source evidence.
- Only current, traceable, buyer-authored demand may enter Priority A or Priority B review.
- Hiring, job-seeker, recruiter, self-promotional, generic content, event, stale, closed and context-free repost records are not actionable warm demand.
- Ambiguous evidence remains research-only.
- Sales Navigator and other cold-fit evidence never becomes confirmed warm intent.
- Every external LinkedIn action remains manual and requires human review.
