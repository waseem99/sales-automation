# Offer and Campaign Engine

This package is the shared commercial definition for Unified Prospecting OS campaigns.

## Current active offers

### FinTech Backend Operations Platform

A hybrid product and managed-implementation offer for FinTech, payments, digital banking, wallets, lending, NBFC, remittance, financial-infrastructure and RegTech accounts.

Supported routes:

- direct buyer;
- channel or implementation partner.

The package does not claim an existing banking deployment, regulatory approval, guaranteed savings or autonomous financial decisions without separate evidence.

### Managed Software and AI Delivery Partnership

A managed service offer for agencies, consultancies, systems integrators, SaaS and product companies that may require white-label, implementation or overflow delivery capacity.

Supported route:

- delivery/overflow partner.

The package does not infer a confirmed capacity problem, unlimited resource availability or approved commercial terms.

## Current campaigns

- `fintech_direct_buyers`
- `fintech_channel_partners`
- `software_ai_overflow_partners`

Each campaign has its own account types, personas, positive terms, first-touch hypothesis, scorecard and no-buyer-intent warning.

## Governing rules

- Cold ICP fit is not buyer intent.
- Separate warm evidence may be linked, but does not erase the cold campaign distinction.
- Offers and campaigns may be active, on hold or retired.
- On-hold campaigns retain searches and history but do not run.
- Only approved proof may be recommended.
- Proof limitations remain visible.
- Every match requires human review.
- No message, email, InMail, connection request, proposal or other external action is performed by this package.

## Browser integration

LinkedIn/Sales Navigator extension 1.5.0 seeds the same three campaign lanes and migrates legacy `fintech_backend_operations` searches into `fintech_direct_buyers`.

Operators may create or duplicate additional campaigns without code changes. A new campaign starts on hold and without registered searches. Existing searches are not copied when duplicating a campaign.
